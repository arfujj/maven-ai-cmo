## What Maven changed

- Rebranded as Maven, an AI CMO; the front door `start-here` became `cmo`.
- Added the CMO layer: `marketing-plan`, `weekly-brief` and `client-report`.
- Added the `qa` agent and wired it into both workflows' QA gates.
- Added a session-start hook that gives Maven the active brand's state.
- Added three routines: weekly CMO brief, monthly local checkup, monthly client report.
- Removed every upsell to the original author's services, community and house tool,
  and replaced them with cadence lines that offer Maven's own routines.
- Refocused the positioning on local service businesses and the agencies that serve them.
- Extended the banned-terms check so the removed offers can't come back.
