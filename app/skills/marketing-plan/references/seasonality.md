# Seasonality by trade

Load when placing bets on the 13-week line. These are common Northern Hemisphere,
US-style patterns: a starting guess, not data. The business's own call log, booking
history and Search Console beat this table, and a free Google Trends check for the
trade's main search in the business's state or metro confirms it in two minutes. Flip
for the Southern Hemisphere; flatten for mild climates.

**The rule that matters more than the table:** build in the slow months, harvest in the
busy ones. Pages, reviews, profile work and email lists take weeks to pay, so they start
before the rush. Ads and offers go live just before demand climbs, not after the phones
are already ringing.

| Trade | Busy | Slow | Plan around |
|---|---|---|---|
| HVAC | Cooling peaks in summer heat waves; heating in the first cold snaps | Spring and fall shoulder months | Sell tune-ups and maintenance plans in the shoulders; have ads ready before the first heat wave and first freeze |
| Plumbing | Freezes (burst pipes), winter water heaters, holiday drains | Fairly steady; late spring is often softer | Emergency demand is weather-driven, so keep the profile and call answering tight all year |
| Roofing | Spring and summer, plus the weeks after hail and wind storms | Deep winter in cold climates | Storm response is a race; reviews and insurance-claim help pages must exist before the storm |
| Landscaping and lawn care | Early spring sign-ups through summer | Winter | Pre-book spring in late winter; offer cleanups in fall; snow work fills winter where it snows |
| Pest control | Spring through late summer | Winter | Recurring plans sold in spring; seasonal pest pages ahead of each wave |
| Pools | Spring openings, summer service | Fall closings, then winter | Opening bookings in late winter; closing offers in late summer |
| Painting, exterior work, decks | Spring to early fall | Winter | Interior work fills winter |
| Movers | Summer, month ends, school breaks | Winter, mid-month | Off-peak pricing to smooth demand |
| Dental | Late in the year as insurance benefits reset, and January | Summer can soften | "Use your benefits before they reset" email in the fall |
| Tax and bookkeeping | January to April | Summer | Year-round bookkeeping offers in summer |
| Family law | January and the weeks after holidays | Late in the year | Pages and reviews ready before January |
| Auto repair | Before winter and before road-trip season | Varies | Seasonal inspections as the offer |
| Locksmith, towing, water damage | Weather and holiday driven, otherwise steady | No true slow season | Answer speed beats every other lever |

For software and online shops, the calendar is budgets and buying cycles (fiscal year
starts, back-to-school, the November and December shopping peak) more than weather.
Ask the owner when their best and worst months were last year; one sentence from them
outranks this file.
