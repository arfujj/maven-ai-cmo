How Maven thinks

Fix the leak before you buy more traffic.

A customer moves through six stages before they become a booked job. Maven finds the one that's leaking most and fixes the leak closest to the money first. More ads into a site that hides the phone number is money spent twice.

The rhythm

A CMO is a cadence, not a single deliverable.

Say "schedule it" after any run and Maven sets up the matching routine. Routines write dated drafts and reports. None of them publish, send or spend.

Each quarter

The 90-day plan

/maven:marketing-plan

One number, where it leaks, three to five ranked bets, the budget split, the season ahead, and the keep-or-kill rule for each bet.

Every Monday

The weekly brief

/maven:weekly-brief

A two-minute memo: the scoreboard against the plan, what shipped, what's waiting on you, and three moves for the week.

Every month

The local checkup

monthly-local-checkup

Your Google profile, review pace, first screen and missed calls, checked against the top 3 in your map pack.

Every month

The review

/maven:performance-review

Real numbers in, lessons out. Every skill applies them next time, so the work gets sharper the longer you use it.

The team behind the CMO

35 skills, one memory.

Every skill reads the same brand memory: your voice, your offer, your customers' own words, your rivals, the plan. You don't have to repeat yourself, and you don't have to pick the skill. Ask in plain words and Maven routes the request.

For agencies

Run twenty local clients like you run one.

  • A folder per client, sealed.Say "new client acme.com" and Maven onboards them in about 3 minutes. A hook blocks any write into the wrong client's folder.
  • One Monday rollup."Brief all clients" writes every client's brief and one table naming the client most at risk.
  • The report owners actually read./maven:client-report writes the month in plain words: the number, what we did, what it did, what we need from you. Honest credit only.
  • Find the next client.find-local-clients ranks the businesses in a trade and town that are losing the most customers. pitch-the-fix writes the short email about one broken thing.

Ground rules

Built to be trusted with a real business.

Drafts, never actions

Nothing is published, sent, scheduled or spent without you. Maven tells you what needs your approval.

No invented numbers

Every figure is measured, sourced or marked as an estimate. Unknown baselines stay unknown until week one counts them.

Honest credit

An ad platform's claims are checked against your own phone log. A pause test settles what the ads really bring in.

Free first

Every skill works with your agent's own web access. API keys deepen the research. You never need one.
